Dual-Mode Design

One Scientific Foundation, Two Distinct Expressions

Orbiting Minds connects quantitative space biology findings with real-time 3D visualization. The system maintains a strict conceptual and technical separation between analytical visual proxies and public storytelling.

Mode 01 Analytical & Empirical

Technical Benchmark Experience

An interactive, data-synchronized murine neural proxy designed to explore spaceflight transcriptomic adaptation. Features dataset-linked analytic evidence, anatomical hotspot annotations, diagnostic wireframe inspection, and a graph-derived PCA background constellation.

  • › Murine proxy geometry: Lissencephalic bilateral cortex and olfactory bulbs.
  • › Three inspection modes: Presentation, Annotated, and Diagnostic topology.
  • › Evidence drawer: Literal vector PCA plots and rRNA-removal QC curves.
  • › Independent asset: Exported to exports/simulation.glb (~1.1 MB).
Mode 02 Narrative & Cinematic

Media Companion: Commander Pip

A stylized zero-G narrative experience following Commander Pip, an astronaut mouse in an orbital research habitat. Designed for public engagement and science communication without compromising the empirical integrity of the primary benchmark data.

  • › Cinematic protagonist: Commander Pip in a tailored EVA space exploration suit.
  • › Zero-G habitat staging: Centrifuge guidance track and Earth porthole horizon.
  • › Cyclic animation loop: Weightless drift, attitude scanning, and tail stabilization.
  • › Independent asset: Exported to exports/media_simulation.glb (~688 KB).

Scientific Grounding

Data Context and Empirical Findings

Source data originates from the NASA Rodent Research-10 (RR-10) mission on the International Space Station, comparing spaceflight (FLT) against ground control (GC) samples.

Cerebellum vs. Hippocampus Profiling

The original comparative transcriptomics analysis evaluated how distinct murine brain structures respond to the microgravity and radiation environment of low Earth orbit:

  • › OSD-563 (Cerebellum): Investigates vestibular adaptation, sensorimotor integration, and regional microvascular gene expression.
  • › OSD-564 (Hippocampus): Analyzes synaptic plasticity, neurogenesis indicators, and cognitive stress response under fluid shift.
  • › Related Identifiers: NASA GeneLab legacy records GLDS-568 and GLDS-569.

Transformation Rules

Data-to-3D Visual Mappings

Explicit boundary rules establishing how quantitative transcriptomic values are translated into 3D scene parameters without misrepresenting biological reality.

Source / Derived Input 3D Visual Encoding Interpretation Boundary
Adaptation / Aggregate Score Core Scale (0.85x – 1.30x) & Emission Warmth Derived visualization control. Stylized abstraction of response magnitude; not a measurement of physical brain volume, edema, or perfusion.
Volatility / Fluid Shift Indicator Cephalic Pulse Rate & Bilateral Asymmetry Derived visualization control. Stylized kinetic metaphor for fluid redistribution; not an intracranial pressure transducer recording.
Cluster Dimensionality Orbital Telemetry Rings (2 – 4) & Probes Interface metaphor. Represents analytic dimensionality and cluster count; not physical instruments orbiting brain tissue.
Sample Timeline Duration Timeline Loop Length (120 – 240 Frames) Standard temporal loop. Standardized playback duration for smooth WebGL rendering.

Scientific Boundaries

Explicit Limitations

Transparent interpretation boundaries to ensure clarity for researchers, reviewers, and the public.

01. Visual Proxy, Not Clinical Model

The technical scene is an interactive benchmark-informed visualization proxy. It is not a validated biomedical simulator, anatomical reconstruction, or clinical diagnostic tool.

02. Authority of Primary Literature

Visual parameters (such as scale factors, emission warmth, pulsation rates, and telemetry ring counts) are qualitative visual encodings. The original Kaggle notebooks, peer-reviewed publications, and NASA OSDR accessions remain the sole authoritative references.

03. Narrative Fiction Boundaries

The Media Companion (Commander Pip: Zero-G Odyssey) is stylized narrative content created for public engagement and visual storytelling; it should not be interpreted as scientific evidence, historical flight reconstruction, or biological simulation.
